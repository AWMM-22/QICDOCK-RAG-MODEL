# QICDOCK Frequently Asked Questions

## Product Questions

### Q: What is MagSafe compatibility?
A: MagSafe is Apple's magnetic charging system. QICDOCK chargers with MagSafe compatibility have built-in magnets that align perfectly with iPhone 12 and later models for secure attachment and optimal charging.

### Q: What is Qi wireless charging?
A: Qi is the universal wireless charging standard. All QICDOCK chargers support Qi charging, making them compatible with any Qi-enabled device including Android phones.

### Q: Do I need a special case for wireless charging?
A: Most thin cases (< 3mm) work fine. Thick cases, metal cases, or cases with magnetic attachments may interfere with charging. QICDOCK chargers work best with MagSafe-compatible cases.

### Q: Can I charge multiple devices at once?
A: Currently, QICDOCK chargers are designed for single-device charging. Each charging pad supports one device at a time.

### Q: What vehicles are supported?
A: QICDOCK offers custom-fit chargers for popular models including Mahindra XUV 3XO, Toyota Glanza, Toyota Taisor, Maruti Suzuki Dzire, Maruti Suzuki Swift, and more. Check product compatibility for your specific vehicle.

### Q: What makes QicDock different?
A: QicDock is the only car-specific, custom-fit Magnetic dock designed to keep your iPhone charging completely hands-free while you're on the road. Engineered to fit seamlessly into the centre console of your car, this innovative charging pad securely snaps your Magnetic-compatible iPhone into place, eliminating the hassle of tangled cables or bulky mounts.

### Q: How does QicDock solve the cable mess problem?
A: Your car may be designed for convenience, but charging your phone often means tangled cables, loose wires, and cluttered consoles. QicDock removes that friction. It keeps your phone charging without the cable hassle, giving it a dedicated place while keeping the console cleaner, simpler, and more organised. Just dock, charge, and drive.

## Installation Questions

### Q: How do I install the charger?
A: Most QICDOCK chargers are designed for tool-free installation. They fit into your vehicle's existing center console or dashboard cavity. Detailed installation guides are included with each product.

### Q: Does installation require wiring?
A: No, QICDOCK chargers typically connect to your car's existing 12V/cigarette lighter socket or USB port. No permanent wiring modifications needed.

### Q: Will it fit my car perfectly?
A: Vehicle-specific models are precision-engineered for exact fit. Universal models have adjustable mounts. Check product compatibility for your vehicle make, model, and year.
