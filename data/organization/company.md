## Brand Information
QICDOCK specializes in precision-fit magnetic wireless charging docks that integrate seamlessly with vehicle center consoles and dashboards. The brand is known for its "Precision Fit" approach, designing products tailored to specific vehicle makes and models.

QicDock, the only car-specific, custom-fit Magnetic dock designed to keep your iPhone charging completely hands-free while you're on the road. Engineered to fit seamlessly into the centre console of your car, this innovative charging pad securely snaps your Magnetic-compatible iPhone into place, eliminating the hassle of tangled cables or bulky mounts.

The Cable Mess We All Know.
Your car may be designed for convenience, but charging your phone often means tangled cables, loose wires, and cluttered consoles. Every drive becomes another round of plugging in, unplugging, and finding somewhere to keep the cable. QicDock removes that friction. It keeps your phone charging without the cable hassle, giving it a dedicated place while keeping the console cleaner, simpler, and more organised.

Just dock, charge, and drive.
