# QICDOCK Policies

## Return Policy

QicDock products can be returned within 7 days from the date of delivery. Customers must raise a return request within this 7-day period.

The product must be returned in its original condition, along with all original packaging, accessories, cables, manuals, and other components.

Once the returned product is received by QicDock, it will be inspected to ensure that it meets the return requirements.

## Refund Policy

Refunds will be processed within 7 business days after QicDock receives and successfully inspects the returned product.

The refund will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

The time taken for the refund to reflect in the customer's account may vary depending on the bank, card issuer, payment gateway, or other financial institution.

Please note: Raising a return request within 7 days of delivery does not mean that the refund is processed immediately. The product must first be received and inspected by QicDock.

## Product Condition Requirements

To be eligible for a return and refund:

- The product must be unused and in its original condition.
- The product must not have scratches, marks, physical damage, or other signs of use.
- All original accessories, cables, manuals, and other components must be included.
- The product must be returned in its original packaging.
- Any promotional gifts or free items included with the order must also be returned, where applicable.
- The returned product must match the product originally purchased.

QicDock reserves the right to reject a return if the product does not meet these requirements.

## Promotional Offers

For orders placed under a promotional offer, bundle offer, discount, or free-gift promotion, the applicable promotional terms will apply.

## Products Purchased Through Third-Party Platforms

For QicDock products purchased through Amazon, Flipkart, or other authorised third-party platforms, the return, refund, and replacement process will be governed by the respective platform's policies.

Customers should initiate the return through the platform from which the product was purchased.

## Warranty

QicDock products are covered under the applicable QicDock warranty against manufacturing and quality-related defects.

The warranty is separate from the 7-day return policy. Once the 7-day return period has expired, eligible quality-related issues will be handled under the applicable warranty terms.

Depending on the nature of the issue, QicDock may provide a repair, replacement, or other applicable resolution under the warranty.

# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Coverage

QicDock currently ships only within India.

We aim to make the delivery process simple, reliable, and convenient for every customer across our serviceable locations in India.

## Shipping Charges

Shipping is completely free on all QicDock orders.

No additional shipping or delivery charges will be added to the order at checkout, subject to the serviceability of the delivery address.

## Order Processing

Once an order is successfully placed, QicDock will process and prepare the order for dispatch within 2 business days.

Orders are processed after successful confirmation of the payment and order details.

Please note that order processing time is separate from the delivery time.

## Delivery Time

Orders are generally delivered within 7-10 days from the date of order confirmation.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on the customer's location, courier availability, weather conditions, public holidays, or other circumstances beyond QicDock's control.

In certain remote or less-accessible locations, delivery may take additional time.

## Order Confirmation

Once your order is successfully placed, you will receive an order confirmation on WhatsApp using the contact details provided at the time of purchase.

The confirmation will contain relevant information about your order and help keep you updated throughout the delivery process.

## Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking details will be provided so that you can follow the status of your shipment.

Customers will be able to track their order through QicDock's designated order-tracking platform.
